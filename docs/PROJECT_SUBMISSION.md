## Summary
NocturneMouse is a bilingual educational Godot game for school-age space enthusiasts. A stylized mouse introduces a library of adventures about hardware on the Moon, Mars and in deep space. The main native adventure, Mars Jelly: Sweet Horizon, lets learners drive an Opportunity-inspired rover through a four-act story about exploration, sunlight, a dust storm and the discoveries left behind. Separate adventures cover lunar laser ranging, Voyager 1 and space heritage.

## How it works
The main story uses a rigged rover, procedural low-poly terrain, collision physics, waypoint objectives, weather transitions and localized dialogue. The library also includes a native illustrated reader and four standalone HTML games. The player can return to the mouse-care scene without resetting care progress. English is the default; Spanish is optional.
